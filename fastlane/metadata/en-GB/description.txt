Safe Crossing helps children aged 4–9 practise crossing the road with a grown-up. Follow a yellow chick and a grown-up through four illustrated towns and practise waiting at red lights, checking for traffic in every direction, watching for turning vehicles and keeping an eye on traffic while crossing.

The 12 short lessons give children two choices at a time and explain each answer. Children can try again after a mistake. A daily task revisits one of the lessons, and each town has an illustrated sign quiz. Finishing a town’s three lessons and its sign quiz earns a dated certificate celebrating in-app practice.

The parent area shows progress and highlights lessons to revisit together. It also offers an optional daily reminder and a family guide that cites pedestrian-safety materials from the US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration. Signs are simplified for learning. Local signs and rules vary, and crossing independently is outside the scope of the lessons.

Available on iPhone and iPad in English, German, French and Brazilian Portuguese.
