# Safe Crossing

## What it is

Safe Crossing is a road-safety learning app for children ages 4–9 and the grown-ups who practise with them. Children help a chick make safe choices in illustrated street scenes, answer questions about signs and signals, and earn an in-app learning certificate for each city they finish.

## Launch and onboarding

1. After the system launch screen, the app opens directly to the “City” tab. The navigation title is “Safe Crossing”. The page reads “LEARN • EXPLORE • PRACTISE”, followed by “Little steps.” and “Safer streets.” on separate lines.
2. On a fresh install, the certificate counter is “0 / 4”. “TODAY’S ROAD TASK” shows the title of the lesson selected for that date and “One small lesson together”. Tapping the card opens that lesson.
3. “Spring” / “Sunny Town” is selected. Its illustrated card shows “0 / 3 lessons complete”. Below “Your learning route” and “01”, “The waiting light” is available with “3 thoughtful choices”. “Zebra crossing” and “Pavement partners” initially show “Finish the previous stop first”. “City sign check” is available immediately.
4. The tab bar offers “City”, “Signs” and “Certificates”. The person icon in the top bar is named “For parents” for VoiceOver. There is no onboarding walkthrough, welcome button or permission prompt at launch.

- “Gentle reminders” reads “An optional daily nudge to practise together. ## Features

- “Your learning route” across four illustrated seasonal cities, each with three sequential, replayable lessons.
- “STREET PRACTICE” with 12 lessons, three choices to make per lesson, and explanations about safe crossing, traffic signals, turning cars, pavement safety, distractions, visibility and slippery conditions.
- “TODAY’S ROAD TASK”, which rotates through the 12 lessons by date.
- “SIGN DISCOVERY” and “City sign check”, with four illustrated questions per city.
- “Certificates”, with one dated “Learning certificate” per city after its three lessons and sign check are complete.
- “For parents” with a sum gate, “Learning overview”, “City report”, “Revisit together” and “A note for your family”.
- “Gentle reminders” with an optional repeating notification and adjustable “Practice time”.
- “Learning streets” widget previews and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.
- “Erase learning progress” to reset local progress, statistics, awards and reminders.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- **Locked stops.** “Finish the previous stop first” means the previous lesson in that city must be completed. The first lesson in each city, every “City sign check” and “TODAY’S ROAD TASK” can open immediately.
- **No way forward after a wrong choice.** “Let’s think together” keeps the question open. Pick the correct answer to see “A thoughtful choice!” and “Continue”. After a correct choice, the answer buttons are disabled until “Continue” is tapped. This includes the final question, where “Continue” is required to record completion.
- **Repeated questions.** “Watch the turning cars” uses “Now the walking signal is on. What comes next?” in two consecutive steps. “Wait for every lane” uses “We are at the crossing. Cars are approaching. What now?” twice in a row. The buttons reverse order on the second step. Choose the correct answer and tap “Continue” each time.
- **The completion button can return to “Signs”.** “Back to the city” closes the current screen. A check opened from “Signs” returns to “Signs”.
- **A completed sign check can leave the certificate card unearned.** “Complete 3 lessons + the city sign check” means all four activities are required, in either order. If the check was completed first, finishing the remaining lessons awards the certificate automatically. Retries affect the attempt count and do not prevent an award.
- **The daily task is still available after completion.** “Today’s task is complete” marks the date’s lesson as done and still allows replay. A new date brings another lesson.
- **The parent gate can reappear.** Enter the correct sum and tap “Open parent area”. Reopening the parent sheet or returning after the app went into the background requires a fresh sum.
- **The report starts at zero and changes with practice.** “Correct choices / attempts” starts at “—” and includes retries once answers are submitted. “City report” lesson rows start at “0 ×” with no activity date. An answer can add a date before the lesson is finished. Replaying and finishing a lesson increases its count, while “Completed lessons” and “Certificates” count unique completions and awards. “Days of learning” increases once per date with a finished lesson or check. “Revisit together” appears after a lesson mistake and retains that lesson after later successful attempts until progress is erased.
- **The reminder can remain off.** If permission is denied, use “Open system settings” to allow notifications, then return and enable “Daily practice reminder”. If “The reminder could not be saved. Please try again.” appears, turn the reminder on again. Lessons remain available either way.
- **Reset sends progress back to the starting state.** Confirming “Erase learning progress” clears counts, dates and awards, relocks later route stops, switches the reminder off and restores the default practice time. “Cancel” dismisses the confirmation.
- **Save failure.** “Progress could not be saved” has a “Close” button that dismisses the alert. The alert has no retry button.

## Starter content and resume

A fresh install includes the four cities, 12 built-in lessons, four sign checks and four unearned certificate cards. It starts with no completed lessons, awards, attempts or learning days, and with the reminder off.

Completed lessons, completed sign checks, awards, attempts, activity dates and reminder settings carry across launches. Closing an unfinished lesson or check discards the current question position. Reopening starts at the first question, with previous answer attempts still recorded. A regular cold launch opens “City” with “Sunny Town” selected.

## Permissions

Optional notification permission for alerts and sounds is requested when a grown-up first enables “Daily practice reminder” in “For parents”. The app supplies no custom system usage-description string. Its on-screen explanation is “An optional daily nudge to practise together. Notification permission is requested only when you turn this on.” The local notification is titled “Safe Crossing” and says “A little road-safety practice together today?” There are no other permission requests.

## Absent

Login or accounts, in-app purchases, ads, analytics, user-generated content, an account deletion flow and an App Tracking Transparency prompt are all absent.

## Data and support

Progress, answer attempts, activity dates and reminder settings stay on the device. The app works offline. Widgets read the same local progress, and reminders are scheduled on the device. “Erase learning progress” clears the local learning record and reminders. Removing the app removes its local progress. There is no in-app Contact or Support control.

## Scanning and health

None. The signs are built-in illustrations. Road-safety guidance is credited in “For parents” under “A note for your family” to NHTSA’s “Pedestrian Safety” and “Pedestrian Safety Rules for the Family”.

## Platform

- iPhone and iPad, with a build minimum of iOS 17.4 on iPhone and iPadOS 17.4 on iPad.
- iPhone supports portrait and both landscape orientations. iPad also supports upside-down portrait.
- The native interface is localized in English, German, French and Brazilian Portuguese, with English as the default. The app name is “Safe Crossing” in all four. The tab labels are “City”, “Signs”, “Certificates” in English, “Stadt”, “Zeichen”, “Urkunden” in German, “Ville”, “Panneaux”, “Certificats” in French, and “Cidade”, “Placas”, “Certificados” in Brazilian Portuguese.
- The same cities, lessons and simplified signs are used across regions. There is no region picker or country-specific rules mode. Date and time displays follow device settings, and the daily task changes with the local date.
